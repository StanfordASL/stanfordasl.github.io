Adele manages lab operations and project coordination for the Autonomous Systems Lab. She joined the Center for Automotive Research at Stanford (CARS) in 2009 and was a senior member of the team that built the Center, before moving into her current role with the Autonomous Systems Lab. Prior to joining CARS, Adele was with IBM for 15 years in technical marketing.
