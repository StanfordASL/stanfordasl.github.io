Larry Cathey is our Research Platform Manager. Leveraging his 30 years of experience as an automotive research engineer which includes 20 years’ experience with ground based real-time visual simulation, Larry assists students with experimental design, technical support and integration activities. Larry worked with Ford in Dearborn, MI for 30 years in R&D and simulation, with RTI/FAAC for 7 years as Senior Technical Advisor for Business Development and has been with Stanford since 2016.
